I have 10–15 hours per week available for remote freelance work, including meetings and administration. Project timelines depend on the agreed scope and current commitments.

Can we begin with a small project?

Yes. The Website & Visibility Review is a focused first engagement. Implementation can follow as a separately agreed project.

What if I need a new website or more than five pages?

Share the requirements and existing materials. New builds, migrations, additional pages and custom integrations receive a separate scope and quote.

No. The work addresses content, usability, consistency and technical access within the agreed scope. Search engines and AI systems control their results; business outcomes need separate measurement.

What about UX research or event support?

Focused UX reviews and program-planning assignments can be scoped separately. Explore my research experience or contact me with the question you need to answer.
