Published book chapter · 2026

Ethical AI Storytelling in Volunteer Tourism: Code Meets Compassion

Question

How can volunteer tourism communication use generative AI while protecting authenticity and cultural sensitivity?

Approach and contribution

A conceptual chapter examining personalized AI-assisted communication and its ethical implications. Coauthored by Mahboubeh Cheraghian and Saeed Vayghan.

Main argument

Personalization creates communication opportunities alongside concerns about bias, cultural sensitivity and authenticity. These are analytical arguments, not measured campaign effects.

Publication record

Impacts of AI on International Volunteering, pages 109–140. IGI Global Scientific Publishing. DOI: 10.4018/979-8-3373-5022-6.ch005. Publisher citation year: 2026.

Evidence and scope

This summary is grounded in the publisher abstract and bibliographic record. It does not claim an experiment, sample size or effectiveness estimate.
