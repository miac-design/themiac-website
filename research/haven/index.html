HAVEN

Helping hotels detect human trafficking together without sharing guest data.

I coauthored HAVEN: Helping Hotels Detect Human Trafficking Together Without Sharing Guest Data with Saeed Vayghan and Mehmet Erdem. The paper has been accepted for publication in ICHRIE Research Reports.

HAVEN stands for Hospitality Anti-trafficking Vigilance and Early-warning Network. The research explores how hotels could learn collectively from booking patterns while keeping individual guest records at each property.

Research translated into a working concept

The paper presents a conceptual framework using federated learning and differential privacy, accompanied by a web demonstration across 12 simulated hotels. It examines hotel operations, human review of alerts, implementation readiness and the ethical questions that a future pilot would need to address.

Accepted for publication in October 2026; publication date pending. The demonstration uses synthetic data. Real-world detection accuracy, fairness and false-positive rates require further evaluation with hotel partners and appropriate ethical oversight.

Research record

Question, approach and contribution

Research question

Could hotels learn collectively from booking patterns while retaining guest records at each property?

Approach

A conceptual framework combining federated learning and differential privacy, illustrated through a web demonstration across 12 simulated hotels. All demonstration data is synthetic.

Contribution and collaboration

Coauthored by Mahboubeh (Mia) Cheraghian, Saeed Vayghan and Mehmet Erdem. The research translates technical concepts into hotel operations, implementation readiness, human review and ethical considerations. Technical implementation is credited to the research team; individual ownership of every component is not claimed.

What the demonstration can establish

It illustrates the proposed architecture and workflow. It does not establish production detection accuracy, fairness or false-positive performance. A real-world pilot requires hotel partners and ethical oversight.
