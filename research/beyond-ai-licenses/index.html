Beyond AI Licenses

How do you know whether an AI tool is actually creating value?

I coauthored Beyond AI Licenses: How to Measure Whether Your AI Is Actually Working with Hamed Alikhani and Sodik Shakirov, in collaboration with Austin AI Hub.

The manuscript draws on a structured questionnaire, interviews and working sessions with 24 AI practitioners. Fieldwork was conducted by an Uzbekistan Business Leaders fellow at the Hub in June 2026.

From evidence to a decision

The paper proposes an AI Value Audit covering governance, workflow velocity, output quality and attributed financial impact. It connects these measures to decisions about renewing, switching, scaling or retiring an AI tool.

2026 manuscript. Small, purposive sample; findings describe the participants rather than the wider market. Publication status has not been verified.

Research record

Question, approach and contribution

Research question

How can a manager decide whether an AI tool in use is creating value rather than merely generating activity?

Study design

A structured questionnaire supplemented by interviews and working sessions with 24 practitioners responsible for adopting or measuring AI. A UBL fellow conducted the June 2026 fieldwork at Austin AI Hub. The sample is purposive and does not represent the wider market.

Proposed framework

The AI Value Audit moves through governance, workflow velocity, output quality and attributed financial impact. It connects evidence to renewal, switching, scaling or retirement decisions.

Interpretation

The four-layer audit is an author-proposed organizing framework presented to practitioners, rather than a structure independently discovered from the survey. Coauthorship is credited to Mahboubeh (Mia) Cheraghian, Hamed Alikhani and Sodik Shakirov. Publication has not been verified.
