Problem · direction · verification · decision

From the brief to a checked implementation

01

Define the problem

Protbyte needed a clearer account of its IT and security infrastructure offer, with connected service navigation, resources and an inquiry path. I set the brief: use the existing service content, preserve the brand identity and make the next step easy to find.

02

Direct the AI workflow

I directed AI-supported competitor comparisons, prototyping, content organization and HTML implementation. I chose Presidio as the main design reference, with ideas from AHEAD and Centre, and specified what to keep: the logo identity, engineer photography, vendor logos, frameworks and Free Sentinel Scan link.

03

Review and correct AI outputs

I reviewed successive prototypes and rejected unsuitable, watermarked and low-quality hero images. I requested corrections when the engineer photo or branding disappeared. The website work included mobile, page, image and button checks; content revisions stayed within the existing service claims.

04

Make decisions

I chose a left-text/right-engineer hero, recognizable vendor logos with a separate framework row, clearer service links and a direct inquiry flow. I aligned LinkedIn with the website. These choices addressed the brief and established a consistent website and LinkedIn presentation.
