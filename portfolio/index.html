Working together

A clear scope, a useful handoff.

  1. Define the decision. Share the problem, target date, budget range and available materials.
  2. Agree on the work. Confirm deliverables, assumptions, access, schedule and review points.
  3. Review and hand off. Discuss findings or progress and receive the agreed outputs, with next steps and limitations.

I have 10–15 hours per week available for remote freelance work, including meetings and administration. Timelines are confirmed against the scope and my current commitments.

Can we begin with a small project?

Yes. A focused review or brief is a practical first engagement. Larger work can be divided into agreed milestones.

What about confidential information?

We agree on access and confidentiality before work starts. My employer’s internal documents and research are not shared as freelance samples.

Will GEO work guarantee AI citations?

No. The work can address content, entity consistency and technical access. Citation and traffic outcomes need separate measurement; AI engines control their responses.
