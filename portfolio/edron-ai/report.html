Brand naming + website design + GEO

EDRON AI: from brand name to digital presence

I created the EDRON AI brand name, contributed to the branding, designed the website, and worked on generative engine optimization to support discovery in search and AI-generated answers.

01 / Overview

Connect brand identity, website experience and discovery

EDRON AI is an Austin-based AI consultancy. My work connected the brand name and digital presentation with a website that introduces its services and helps prospective clients learn more.

02 / My contribution

What I created and supported

  • Created the EDRON AI brand name.
  • Contributed to branding and digital presentation.
  • Designed the EDRON AI website.
  • Applied GEO techniques to support discovery in search and AI-generated answers.

03 / Outcome

A public website and a coherent brand presence

The live website presents EDRON AI’s strategy, agents, academy and visibility services. It provides a public example of my website and branding work.

The GEO work focused on improving discoverability. Search ranking, AI citation, traffic and lead-generation improvements have not been quantified in this case study.
