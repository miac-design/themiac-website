What was unclear

The project needed a coherent way to introduce an AI consultancy: a brand name, a digital identity, a website and understandable routes into its services.

02

What I learned

Naming, positioning and the website needed to tell the same story. The public service structure—strategy, agents, academy and visibility—provided a concrete basis for organizing the offer.

03

What I changed

I created the EDRON AI name, contributed to the branding, designed the website and worked on GEO to support discovery in search and AI-generated answers.

04

Why

Connecting the name, service structure and digital presentation gives prospective clients a consistent introduction to the business. This explains the design intent; changes in traffic, citations and inquiries have not been quantified.
