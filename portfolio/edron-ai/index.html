The reasoning behind the digital presence

01

Define the problem

The consultancy needed a coherent introduction: a brand name, digital identity and website that explained its services. I connected naming and website design around that need.

02

Direct the work

I created the EDRON AI name, contributed to the branding and directed the website design and GEO work. The service structure—strategy, agents, academy and visibility—provided a basis for organizing the offer.

03

Keep the evidence clear

The public website is the inspectable implementation. This case records my naming, branding, design and GEO contribution; it does not substitute generated content for customer research or claim a measured search improvement.

04

Make decisions

I connected the name, service structure and digital presentation to give prospective clients a consistent introduction. The rationale is clarity and coherence; traffic, AI citations and lead-generation lift have not been quantified.
