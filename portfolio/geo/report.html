Austin AI Hub: GEO audit and implementation

Executive summary

Building a consistent organization identity

The project brought content, entity consistency and technical access together to improve the foundation for AI-search discovery. My work combined audit, factual content development and technical implementation. No measured citation or traffic increase is claimed.

GEO implementation

Making the organization easier to find

The project

The organization needed clear, consistent information that search engines and AI assistants could read and verify.

My role

I worked on the website and its generative engine optimization, combining an audit of the organization’s online presence with content and technical changes.

  • Standardized organization details and corrected inconsistent information.
  • Worked with JSON-LD structured data, including organization and FAQ content.
  • Addressed crawler access through robots.txt, alongside sitemap and canonical-tag work.
  • Developed factual About content and direct-answer FAQs.
  • Reviewed external entity profiles and authority signals.

Outcome and context

This work strengthened the site’s content and machine-readable organization information. AI-citation and traffic lift have not yet been measured with a comparable baseline. The work was pro bono, and Austin AI Hub and EDRON AI share leadership.

Evidence and limitations

This record summarizes documented implementation work. A consistent historical citation baseline is unavailable. Readiness scores are not treated as evidence of a measured performance increase. The engagement was pro bono, and Austin AI Hub and EDRON AI share leadership.

Next steps

Establish a dated prompt set, named engines and scoring criteria; repeat observations after changes and record model versions. Separate factual accuracy, citations and website traffic rather than treating them as one metric.
