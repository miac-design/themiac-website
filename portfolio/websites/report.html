My personal website

Website design and documentation report

Executive summary

The question and the contribution

How do you let a first-time visitor understand the work quickly, while giving a deeper reader enough evidence and detail?

I designed and built my personal website to connect my professional experience, research and project work, using UX judgment and AI-assisted development to turn ideas into working pages.

Deliverables

  • Personal website design and implementation
  • Project-card portfolio and individual case studies
  • Detailed project reports
  • Responsive layouts and contact paths
  • AI-assisted development with Claude and ChatGPT

Impact

The live site gives visitors a route from selected work to case-study summaries and detailed reports. It is an inspectable website example with public source code. Visitor conversion and usability improvement have not been measured.

Project record / Responsibilities and process

The project

A personal website should make someone’s work easy to understand and give visitors a clear next step.

My role

I designed and built themiac.com as a home for my research, professional experience and projects. This portfolio is part of that work. I use AI-assisted development to turn my ideas into working pages, combining my understanding of user experience with hands-on website creation.
