01 / Scope

Building the organization’s public presence

As a founding team member, I helped name and brand Austin AI Hub, built its website, managed social media and announcements, planned events, photographed events myself and developed partnerships. I became CMO in June 2026.

02 / Delivery decision

Connect identity, information and participation

A new nonprofit needs people to understand what it does and how to participate. My work connected its name and branding with website content, event announcements and community communications.

The website explains the mission, programs and routes into the community. Event photography documents the people behind the organization. This is the purpose of the connected approach; recognition or conversion improvements have not been quantified.
