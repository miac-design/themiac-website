Project record / Responsibilities and process

The project

Austin AI Hub was being built from the ground up. The work involved establishing its identity, building its website, creating a public presence and organizing the programs that would bring its community together.

My role

As a founding team member, I worked across the organization from its early stages. I helped name and brand Austin AI Hub, built its website, managed social media and event communications, planned events and photographed them myself. I also developed partnerships and led the production and community work behind its global hackathon. In June 2026, I became Chief Marketing Officer.

  • Built Austin AI Hub’s website and developed the content that explains its mission, programs and ways to participate.
  • Helped establish the organization’s name, branding and public identity as part of the founding team.
  • Planned community events and handled their announcements, social media promotion and participant communications.
  • Photographed Austin AI Hub events myself, documenting the people, activities and community behind the organization.

Outcome and context

My contribution spans the organization’s identity, digital presence and on-the-ground programs. The website, communications, events and photography work together to make Austin AI Hub recognizable and help people find a way into its community.

GEO implementation

Making the organization easier to find

The project

The organization needed clear, consistent information that search engines and AI assistants could read and verify.

My role

I worked on the website and its generative engine optimization, combining an audit of the organization’s online presence with content and technical changes.

  • Standardized organization details and corrected inconsistent information.
  • Worked with JSON-LD structured data, including organization and FAQ content.
  • Addressed crawler access through robots.txt, alongside sitemap and canonical-tag work.
  • Developed factual About content and direct-answer FAQs.
  • Reviewed external entity profiles and authority signals.

Outcome and context

This work strengthened the site’s content and machine-readable organization information. AI-citation and traffic lift have not yet been measured with a comparable baseline. The work was pro bono, and Austin AI Hub and EDRON AI share leadership.
