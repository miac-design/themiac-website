Building a nonprofit’s identity and digital presence

01 / Overview

How do you make a new organization recognizable, discoverable and easy for people to join?

The nonprofit needed a recognizable identity, a useful website and consistent ways for people to discover programs and participate.

02 / Deliverables

What I worked on

  • Organization naming and brand development
  • Complete website and program content
  • Event planning and community communications
  • Photography I created at Hub events
  • GEO audit, content and technical improvements

03 / Impact and evidence

The organization has a public identity, a working website and community programs. My founding contribution developed into the CMO role in June 2026. GEO changes strengthened content and machine-readable information; comparable citation and traffic lift have not yet been measured.
