The project

A global challenge needed a website, a clear path from registration to submission, and a team to bring the program and awards event together.

My role

I was the lead producer of the hackathon, carrying the program from initiation through execution. My work connected the website, program design, partnerships, outreach, participant communications, judging workflows and awards event. I built the hackathon website in full and worked directly with Call for Code AI on announcement reviews and public communications.

  • Built the entire hackathon website, giving participants a place to understand the challenge, register and find program information.
  • Designed the operational blueprint, including challenge milestones, submission screening, judging workflows and mentor support.
  • Led partnership coordination, community outreach, event promotion and participant communications across the program.
  • Led event planning and production through the awards ceremony, bringing the program team, partners and community together.

Design and delivery choices

The participant journey brings registration, challenge tracks, workshop resources and submission guidance into one website. The operational blueprint uses a screening stage before panel judging and a shared support channel to organize participant questions. These are concrete parts of the program design, rather than a website alone.

Outcome and context

The program reached its awards event on July 30, 2026, bringing an international community together around AI for social good. I am quoted in both published Call for Code AI announcements: the April 30 launch and the July 30 winners announcement. The launch release also lists me as an Austin AI Hub media contact. The winners announcement reports participation from dozens of countries across six continents. The impact report identifies five teams selected for a six-month incubator, with the remaining honored teams invited to join. The accelerator is the next phase of the work and remains underway.
