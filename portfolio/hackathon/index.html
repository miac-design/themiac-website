01 / Overview

How do you give a global community a clear path from interest to participation, submission and recognition?

I led the production of Call for Code AI: United Against Trafficking, connecting the program, entire website, partnerships, participant communications, judging operations and awards event.

02 / Deliverables

What I worked on

I authored the concept proposal that led to Call for Code AI: United Against Trafficking, a global initiative hosted by Austin AI Hub and presented by Call for Code AI and UN Human Rights.

  • Hackathon concept proposal and partnership development
  • Complete hackathon website
  • Program milestones and operational blueprint
  • Partner and participant communications
  • Judging and mentor workflows
  • Awards-event planning and production

03 / Impact and evidence

What the work achieved

The program brought participants from six continents together and reached its awards ceremony on July 30, 2026. Its impact report records 32 judges, more than 400 written evaluations and 10 honored teams. These are program outcomes, not results attributed solely to me.

Evidence

Two published Call for Code AI announcements and the Austin AI Hub impact report.

My contribution

Ownership and collaboration

I led production and built the entire website. Partners, judges, mentors and participants contributed to the wider program. The reported program scale is a collective outcome.
