01 / Scope

Research across product and marketing

My work connects customer needs, market evidence and competitive intelligence with decisions about offerings, messaging and positioning. Stakeholders include presidents, the CMO, and product and marketing directors.

02 / Research decision

Match the method to the business question

The practice begins by clarifying which decision the stakeholder needs to make. A customer-experience question, a market-positioning question and a sales objection call for different evidence and different outputs.

QuestionResearch contributionDecision-facing deliverable
What do users need?UX studies using interviews, surveys and other appropriate methods.Findings and prioritized recommendations.
Where does the offering fit?Market and competitive studies.Evidence supporting positioning and differentiation.
How can sales explain the value?Competitive synthesis and messaging research.Competitor battlecards and sales-enablement material.
