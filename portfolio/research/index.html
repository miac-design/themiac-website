Research ownership

My role in the decision process

Frame
Clarify the business question and the decision the research needs to support with executive, product and marketing stakeholders.
Investigate and synthesize
Conduct UX, market and competitive studies; translate findings into recommendations, competitor battlecards and sales-enablement material.
Inform the decision
I own the research contribution. Business owners make the product, marketing and commercial decisions. Confidential study artifacts and results stay within Rackspace.

01 / Overview

How can research turn a complex business or technology question into evidence that people can act on?

The work connects a stakeholder’s question with appropriate methods and usable outputs: customer findings, positioning recommendations and sales-enablement material.

02 / Deliverables

What I worked on

  • UX, market and competitive studies
  • Executive findings and recommendations
  • Competitor battlecards and sales-enablement material
  • Analyst-relations support through research narratives, briefings and inquiry responses involving Forrester, IDC and Gartner

03 / Impact and evidence

How the research is used

My research supports product and marketing decisions, messaging, positioning and sales enablement. I work with executive stakeholders including presidents, the CMO, and product and marketing directors. Business results and study details remain confidential.
